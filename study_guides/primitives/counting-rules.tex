\section*{Practice problems}

\begin{problem}
[Calibration placeholder — replaced in phase 2 under the solve gate.]
Three lights each show one of three colours, independently. How many
distinct displays can the panel show?
\begin{hint}
One stage per light; independent stages multiply.
\end{hint}
\begin{solution}
Each light contributes a factor of $3$ and the stages are independent, so
the multiplicative rule gives $3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$ distinct displays.
(Same shape as the raw CTC path count $3^4 = 81$ the alignment chapter
builds on this rule.)
\end{solution}
\end{problem}
