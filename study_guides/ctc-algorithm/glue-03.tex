\section*{The road so far}

\roadmap{3}

\noindent The move has a name now, and the grid is honest about what it computes. What it is not honest about yet is what it multiplies: a path's weight is a product of per-frame scores, taken on faith. Whether the world permits that multiplication — when a joint chance really is a product of separate chances — is not bookkeeping; it is the first real claim about probability this road makes, and the next chapter builds the square that decides it.
