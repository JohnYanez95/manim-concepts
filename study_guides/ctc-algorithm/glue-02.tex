\section*{The road so far}

\roadmap{2}

\noindent You watched a sum too wide to list get priced on twenty nodes, and you watched the chapter borrow: those per-frame scores are still unearned. Before the road starts repaying, one short chapter stops to name the trick that just happened — because the trellis was not a lucky arrangement of circles.
