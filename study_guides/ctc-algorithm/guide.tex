\newcommand{\roadmapstations}{%
  counting, alignment, dyn.~prog., independence, conditional, Bayes,
  logarithms, {$e$ \& $\ln$}, random~vars, softmax, derivatives,
  descent, gradient, decoding}
\newcommand{\roadmap}[1]{%
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tikzpicture}[x=1.9cm, y=1.15cm,
      station/.style={rounded rectangle, draw=Muted, font=\tiny,
        inner sep=3pt, minimum height=4.5mm},
      done/.style={station, fill=GoodInk!25, draw=GoodInk},
      hop/.style={Muted, ->, shorten >=2pt, shorten <=2pt}]
    \foreach [count=\i] \name in \roadmapstations {
      \pgfmathtruncatemacro{\row}{(\i - 1) / 7}
      \pgfmathtruncatemacro{\col}{mod(\i - 1, 7)}
      \ifnum\i>#1
        \node[station] (s\i) at (\col, -\row) {\name};
      \else
        \node[done] (s\i) at (\col, -\row) {\name};
      \fi
      \ifnum\i>1
        \pgfmathtruncatemacro{\prev}{\i - 1}
        \ifnum\col=0
          \draw[hop] (s\prev.south) to[out=-60, in=120] (s\i.north);
        \else
          \draw[hop] (s\prev.east) -- (s\i.west);
        \fi
      \fi
    }
  \end{tikzpicture}
  \end{center}}

machinery, the loss, the gradient, the training dynamics, and decoding.
The road runs through fourteen stations; each is a chapter, and between
chapters a short transition marks where you stand:

\noindent The order is the dependency order: the CTC chapters pose the
problem early, the middle chapters build the blocks it needs, and the
closing chapters spend them. Practice problems close every chapter;
worked solutions live in the separate solutions manual, keyed by the
same problem numbers. Worked numbers are spliced from the repo's
verification anchors — for instance, the guide's central worked
probability $P(\mathrm{AB} \mid X) = \anchor{010.K.P}$ — never retyped.
